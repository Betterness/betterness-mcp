# Women's Health

## Description
Skills addressing the hormonal and physiological needs specific to female biology, male hormone optimization, and pregnancy wellness. Female hormone balance uses cycle-phase context to personalize training, nutrition, and recovery recommendations. Pregnancy wellness applies trimester-specific biomarker reference ranges. Male hormone optimizer covers testosterone and its relationship with sleep, training, and body composition.

## Skills

| Skill | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `female-hormone-balance` | Cycle-aware hormone analysis and optimization |
| `pregnancy-wellness` | Trimester-specific biomarker monitoring and wellness support during pregnancy |
| `male-hormone-optimizer` | Evidence-based male hormone optimization protocol |

## Typical User Profile
- Women tracking menstrual cycles and experiencing cycle-related performance variation
- Women managing hormonal imbalances, fatigue, or thyroid concerns
- Pregnant women seeking data-driven pregnancy wellness monitoring
- Men experiencing low energy, reduced libido, or performance declines
- Anyone who has received a hormone panel and wants clinical-quality interpretation
- Fitness-focused individuals connecting hormone health to training outcomes
