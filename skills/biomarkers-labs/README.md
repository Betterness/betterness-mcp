# Biomarkers & Labs

## Description
Skills for interpreting laboratory results with clinical context, identifying patterns across panels, and connecting biomarker findings to wearable and lifestyle data. Covers cardiovascular risk, liver and kidney function, thyroid health, chronic inflammation, iron deficiency, vitamin D, and immune function. Uses optimal ranges (not just standard lab normals) and cross-references results with activity, sleep, and body composition.

## Skills

| Skill | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `biomarker-decoder` | Plain-English biomarker interpretation with clinical context |
| `cardio-assessment` | Multi-factor cardiovascular risk assessment |
| `heart-health-scorecard` | Comprehensive cardiovascular health score across lipids, inflammation, fitness, metabolic, and recovery domains |
| `liver-health-check` | Liver enzyme analysis and NAFLD risk assessment with lifestyle correlation |
| `kidney-function-monitor` | Track creatinine, eGFR, and electrolytes to monitor kidney health over time |
| `thyroid-deep-dive` | Comprehensive thyroid cascade analysis |
| `inflammation-tracker` | Chronic inflammation detection and resolution protocol |
| `iron-deficiency-detective` | Detect early-stage iron deficiency before it becomes anemia |
| `vitamin-d-tracker` | Monitor vitamin D levels and correlate with sleep, immunity, and bone health |
| `immune-system-check` | Evaluate immune readiness from white blood cell counts, nutrients, sleep, and exercise |

## Typical User Profile
- Gets annual or more frequent blood panels
- Wants deeper interpretation than "your results are normal"
- May have borderline markers their doctor dismissed
- Uses a wearable to cross-reference lab findings with lifestyle data
- Interested in optimal ranges and functional medicine perspectives
- May be investigating unexplained fatigue, poor performance, or chronic symptoms
