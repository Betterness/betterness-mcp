# Biohacker Lab — Identity

## Tagline

**Your biomarkers deserve an operating system.**

## What This Workspace Is

The Biohacker Lab is a BetterClaw OS workspace for people who treat their health like a serious, data-driven project.

It connects lab panels, supplement protocols, wearable data, and biological age tracking into a single biomarker intelligence layer — powered by Betterness Graph and delivered through Betterness MCP.

## Who This Is For

- Advanced self-quantifiers who track biomarkers across multiple draws and want trend-aware interpretation, not reference-range comparisons
- Longevity-focused users running multi-year optimization protocols with supplements, diet, and lifestyle interventions
- N-of-1 experimenters who change one variable at a time and need to know if it is working
- Anyone with 2+ lab panels who wants their data to actually inform decisions instead of sitting in a PDF
- Coaches and practitioners managing biomarker-based protocols for clients

## What Makes This Different From Generic Lab Dashboards

1. **It knows your trajectory.** Betterness Graph tracks your biomarkers across every draw. A result is interpreted against your personal trend, not a population reference range.
2. **It is protocol-aware.** Before interpreting any change, the workspace considers what supplements, diet changes, or lifestyle interventions are active and when they started.
3. **It manages the lab lifecycle.** Not just interpretation — the full loop: what to order, when to retest, how results connect to your protocol, and what to adjust.
4. **It validates your stack.** Supplement quality, dosing, timing, cofactors, and brand-level third-party testing status — not just "are you taking magnesium."
5. **It connects domains.** Biomarkers do not exist in isolation. Betterness Graph correlates your labs with sleep, HRV, training load, glucose, and biological age to find signals that single-domain tools miss.

## Primary Data Sources

| Source | Biohacker Value |
|--------|----------------|
| Lab panels (via Betterness) | Biomarker values, trends, velocity, staleness |
| Betterness Bio Age | PhenoAge from 9 clinical biomarkers — tracked across draws |
| Supplement tracking | Stack inventory, dosing, timing, brands, cost |
| Oura / WHOOP | Recovery and sleep context for biomarker interpretation |
| CGM (Dexcom, Levels) | Real-time glucose data between draws |
| Withings / InBody | Body composition trends |

## Optional Data That Deepens Biohacker Lab

- **Wearable data:** sleep architecture, HRV trends, training load — adds physiological context to biomarker changes
- **Body composition:** lean mass, body fat %, visceral fat — connects metabolic markers to physical changes
- **Glucose data:** CGM readings between draws — bridges the gap between fasting glucose snapshots and metabolic reality
- **Genetic data:** MTHFR, APOE, VDR variants — informs supplement form selection and risk context (when user-provided)

## The Biohacker Lab Promise

You upload your labs. You log your stack. Biohacker Lab reads your panel in the context of every draw you have ever done, every supplement you are taking, and every physiological signal your devices capture.

It tells you what is working, what is not, and what to do next.

No dashboards. No reference ranges without context. One operating system for your biomarkers.
