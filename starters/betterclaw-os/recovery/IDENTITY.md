# Recovery Operator — Identity

## Tagline

**Your nervous system has a story. Recovery reads it.**

## What This Workspace Is

The Recovery Operator is a BetterClaw OS workspace for people who take rest as seriously as they take training.

It connects wearable data, sleep architecture, HRV trends, and optionally lab biomarkers into a single recovery intelligence layer — powered by Betterness Graph and delivered through Betterness MCP.

## Who This Is For

- Athletes who need to know when to push and when to pull back
- People recovering from overtraining, burnout, or chronic under-recovery
- Biohackers who track sleep and want actionable interpretation, not raw exports
- Anyone with Oura, WHOOP, Eight Sleep, Apple Watch, or Garmin who wants their recovery data to actually do something
- Coaches managing athlete readiness across a roster

## What Makes This Different From Generic Sleep Apps

1. **It connects to your real data.** Not a questionnaire. Not a mood diary. Your actual wearable signals, processed through Betterness.
2. **It knows your baseline.** Betterness Graph tracks your personal patterns over time — your HRV is compared to your own history, not a population average.
3. **It gates decisions.** Recovery isn't a score to look at. It is a gate that determines what training, intensity, and schedule make sense today.
4. **It gets better over time.** The longer Betterness Graph has context, the more personalized recovery intelligence becomes.
5. **It takes action.** Bedtime reminders, rest day plans, provider suggestions, lab ordering when biomarkers are relevant.

## Primary Devices

| Device | Recovery Value |
|--------|---------------|
| Oura Ring | Sleep stages, HRV, readiness, temperature, SpO2 |
| WHOOP | Recovery score, strain, HRV, respiratory rate |
| Eight Sleep | Sleep tracking, temperature regulation, bed presence |
| Apple Watch | Sleep, heart rate, respiratory rate, blood oxygen |
| Garmin | Sleep, Body Battery, HRV, stress, training load |
| Withings | Sleep tracking, breathing disturbances, heart rate |

## Optional Data That Deepens Recovery

- **Lab biomarkers:** cortisol, testosterone, free T, DHEA-S, thyroid (TSH, fT3, fT4), CRP, ferritin, vitamin D, magnesium
- **Biological age:** trends from Betterness bio age engine
- **Training load:** from connected Garmin, Strava, or TrainingPeaks
- **Supplements:** magnesium, ashwagandha, melatonin, glycine, L-theanine — tracked through Betterness

## The Recovery Operator Promise

You connect your devices once. Recovery Operator reads your overnight data every morning and tells you:
- how you recovered
- why
- what to do about it

No dashboards. No data dumps. One verdict. One plan. Every day.
